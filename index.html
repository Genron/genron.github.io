03 / About

ENGINEERING
WITH INTENT.

I care about the distance between an idea and something a person can actually use.

I am Marco Sanfratello, a fullstack software engineer. My work sits between product, design and engineering: understanding the problem, choosing the right level of complexity, and shipping software that holds up after the prototype phase.

The tools change. The principles stay fairly simple: make it useful, make it understandable, and leave the codebase better than you found it.
Focus Fullstack product engineering
Approach Systems + user experience
Currently exploring Generative interfaces & 3D web
